# Knowledge

## Definition
A fact, idea, or understanding.

Knowledge is semantic. It is what remains after you remove the file.

It answers:
> "What do I know?"

## Examples
- Newton's Second Law
- "Mrs. Smith allows late work."
- Summary of Chapter 6
- Vocabulary definitions
- Meeting minutes
- A brainstorm
- A theorem
- A recipe
- An AI-generated summary
- An extracted transcript

## Not knowledge
- The PDF containing the lecture.
- A JPEG.
- A task.
- A due date.

## Intrinsic properties
- Content
- Confidence
- Source attribution
- Creation time
- Revision history

Knowledge can exist without any artifact.

For example:
> "I had an idea in the shower."

No file exists.
