# Record

## Definition

A record is a statement that something happened.

It is immutable historical truth. Unlike a commitment, which models the future, a record models reality.

It answers:
> "What happened?"

A record may describe something that lasted seconds, hours, or months. It may describe an action, an observation, or an outcome.

## Examples
- Chemistry class from 8:00–8:50.
- Homework assigned.
- Essay submitted.
- Studied calculus for 45 minutes.
- Missed robotics meeting.
- AI conversation occurred.
- Teacher announced a quiz.
- Read Chapter 6.
- Piano practice.
- Received exam grade.
- School canceled due to snow.

Notice these are all things that actually occurred, regardless of whether they were planned.

## Non-examples
- Homework due Friday. (Commitment)
- Newton's Second Law. (Knowledge)
- Chemistry.pdf. (Artifact)
- "This summary references this PDF." (Relationship)

## Intrinsic properties
- Description
- Timestamp or time interval
- Confidence
- Provenance (user, AI, imported, etc.)
- Revision history (if corrections are allowed)

A record never has a "due date."
Reality cannot be due.
